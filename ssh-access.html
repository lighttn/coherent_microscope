Ubuntu Lab PC – SSH Access Guide

Please follow the steps below.

1. Connect to the VUB Network

Install the Ivanti VPN application and log in using your VUB email address and password.

If your computer is connected directly to the VUB network via a LAN cable, a VPN connection may not be required.

2. Open a Terminal

Windows: Open PowerShell or Windows Terminal.
macOS/Linux: Open Terminal.

3. Connect to the Ubuntu Lab PC

Use the username and IP address provided by the administrator:

ssh username@IP_ADDRESS

Example:

ssh student1@134.xxx.xxx.xxx

4. Confirm the Connection

When connecting for the first time, you may see:

Are you sure you want to continue connecting (yes/no/[fingerprint])?

If the IP address is the one provided by the administrator, type:

yes

and press Enter.

5. Enter Your Password

Enter the temporary password provided by the administrator.

Note: No characters will appear on the screen while typing your password. This is normal.

6. Change Your Temporary Password

After logging in for the first time, change your password:

passwd

Enter your temporary password, followed by your new password twice.

7. Access Your Folders

Your private workspace is your home directory:

cd ~

which corresponds to:

/home/username/

Shared laboratory data are available under:

/data/

For example:

/data/raw/          Raw experimental data
/data/projects/     Project data
/data/shared/       Shared files

8. Transfer Files

To upload a file from your computer to the Lab PC:

scp myfile.py username@IP_ADDRESS:/home/username/

To download a file from the Lab PC:

scp username@IP_ADDRESS:/home/username/result.tif .

To transfer an entire directory, use -r:

scp -r username@IP_ADDRESS:/data/projects/FPM .

9. Finish Your SSH Session

When you have finished your work:

exit

This safely closes the SSH connection.
